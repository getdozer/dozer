# Dozer Everyday Zero (`card_basic`)
- **Overview**: No-annual-fee starter card designed for everyday purchases and credit building.
- **Perks & Coverage**: Free monthly FICO credit score tracking, zero fraud liability, and extended warranty protection on electronics.
- **Restrictions**: Charges a 3.0% foreign transaction fee. Does not include airport lounge access or travel delay reimbursement.

# Dozer Cashback Preferred (`card_cashback_plus`)
- **Overview**: High-yield grocery and dining cashback card for households and foodies.
- **Perks & Coverage**: 4% cashback on supermarkets/groceries, 3% on dining and delivery, 2% on travel, and 0% foreign transaction fees. Includes cell phone protection up to $600 and primary rental car collision coverage.
- **Restrictions**: Requires a minimum credit score of 670 and utilization at or below 35%. Does not include Priority Pass lounge membership.

# Dozer Sapphire Voyager (`card_sapphire_voyager`)
- **Overview**: Premier international travel and dining rewards card with airport lounge perks.
- **Perks & Coverage**: 4x points on airlines and hotels, 3x on global dining, $100 Global Entry / TSA PreCheck credit, Priority Pass Select airport lounge access (up to 10 visits/year + $150 annual travel statement credit), trip cancellation/interruption insurance up to $10,000, and 0% foreign transaction fees.
- **Restrictions**: Requires a minimum credit score of 720, annual income of $80,000+, and credit utilization at or below 25%.

# Dozer Zenith Reserve (`card_zenith_reserve`)
- **Overview**: Ultra-luxury flagship card for frequent international flyers and high-spend executives.
- **Perks & Coverage**: 5x points on all travel and flights, 4x on fine dining, unlimited complimentary Priority Pass + Zenith Centurion Lounge access with 2 guests, $300 annual travel credit, complimentary Marriott & Hilton Gold elite status, Global Entry credit, and primary emergency medical evacuation insurance.
- **Restrictions**: Strict underwriting requires a minimum credit score of 760, annual income of at least $130,000, and credit utilization at or below 15%.
